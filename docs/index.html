How Fed pricing is computed
  • ZQ (30-day fed funds futures) price = 100 − that month's average EFFR.
  • Meeting months: monthly average = (days before × old rate + days after × new rate) ÷ days in month, solved for the post-meeting rate.
  • If fewer than 7 days remain after the meeting, the next month's contract is used instead, since that whole month is at the post-meeting rate.
  • Meetings are chained: each post-meeting rate is the next meeting's starting point, starting from today's EFFR.
  • Each meeting's implied move ÷ 25bp is split between the two nearest outcomes, then chained from today's target range to get the probability of each range after each meeting (the same method as CME FedWatch). Futures only give the average expectation, so the distribution shape is an assumption, and prices include a small risk premium.
  • ZQ prices come from Yahoo Finance (unofficial, delayed); compare with CME FedWatch.
